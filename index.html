Company Overview

Who We Are

Cathdon Catering Services is a Nairobi-based professional catering company offering fully managed food service solutions for organizations.

We combine culinary expertise, strict food safety compliance, and structured operational systems to deliver a consistent, high-quality dining experience for our clients' teams, every day.

We work with organizations that need dependable, well-run food service, from daily staff canteens to one-off corporate events.

Our Story

Built on Consistency, Care & Operational Discipline

From serving offices around Nairobi to managing structured corporate and institutional food service across Kenya.

Cathdon Catering Services began with a simple idea: that the people who work hardest for an organization deserve to be fed well.

What started as a small, hands-on catering operation serving offices around Nairobi has grown into a structured, professionally run catering company built on that same principle: good food, prepared with care and served reliably, keeps teams happier, healthier, and more productive.

Today, Cathdon combines that original hospitality mindset with modern food safety systems, disciplined procurement, and transparent reporting. As we've grown, our focus hasn't changed: give every organization we serve a catering partner they can trust to show up, every day, without compromise.
